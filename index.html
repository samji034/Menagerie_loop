menagerie_loop

A personal content-automation tool, built and operated by an individual developer.

What this is

menagerie_loop is a small, single-user pipeline that generates short-form pet/animal-themed video clips and publishes them to social accounts that the developer personally owns or has been explicitly granted access to. It is not a public product, is not distributed to other users, and does not offer any service to third parties.

What it connects to

The tool uses TikTok's Content Posting API and the YouTube Data API, under OAuth authorization explicitly granted by the account holder, solely to upload and publish video content to that same account. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail on what is accessed and what isn't.
