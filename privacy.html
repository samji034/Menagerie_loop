Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026

menagerie_loop is a personal, single-user automation tool. It is not a public service, does not have its own user accounts, and does not collect data from anyone other than the one TikTok and YouTube account it has been explicitly authorized to publish to.

What it accesses

PlatformScopeUsed for
TikTok video.publish Uploading and publishing video content to the authorizing account only.
YouTube youtube.upload Uploading video content (as Shorts) to the authorizing channel only.

What it does not do

  • It does not read, collect, or store any other users' data, content, or activity on either platform.
  • It does not use data for advertising, analytics, or profiling of any kind.
  • It does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.
  • It does not track visitors to this website — this page sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Where data is stored

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the developer's own machine, in order to keep publishing to the authorized account without requiring a fresh login every run. They are never transmitted anywhere other than directly to TikTok's or Google's own API endpoints.

Revoking access

The account holder can revoke the tool's access at any time from TikTok's or Google's own account security settings, which immediately and permanently invalidates the stored tokens.

Contact

Questions, or a request to confirm deletion of stored tokens, can be sent to picanse.be@gmail.com.
